代轉學生會公告】5/10-11 洄潮計畫音樂祭活動管制通知5/10-11 Music Festival Event Control Notice

2025/05/07 11:29 ·
活動公告 ·
學務處課外活動組 info 
各位同學您好!
2025東華大學洄潮計畫音樂祭將於5月10日（六）、5月11日（日）每日11:00至21:15，在大草皮盛大舉行。活動採雙舞台設置，兩天將邀請宋德鶴、海豚刑警、吾橋有水、老王樂隊、裝咖人、P!SCO、無妄合作社、八青哥、粗大Band、拍謝少年、紙鳶、沒有才能等知名樂團，以及東華大學熱音社、慈濟大學熱音社帶來精彩演出。
活動表演將持續至晚間21:15，雖活動場地與宿舍區有一定距離，但每位同學對聲音的接受程度不一，若因此造成不便，敬請見諒。

為配合本次大型活動，校內部分空間與停車場將進行以下管制：
圖書館：5月10日（六）至5月11日（日）閉館。如有自習需求，同學可前往Ｋ書中心或管理學院C109教室使用。
停車場：行政大樓汽機車停車場、行政大樓機車臨時停車場將於5月10日0時至5月11日24時進行場地管制。請需移車的同學於5月9日23:59前，將愛車移至人一汽車停車場、前門機車停車場等其他處停放。

造成不便，敬請見諒，也感謝大家的配合與支持！

Hello students 
2025 Dong Hwa University Music Festival will be held on 10th May (Sat) and 11th May (Sun) from 11:00 to 21:15 daily at the Grand Turf. The event will feature a double stage setup, with 12 renowned indie bands and popular music clubs invited to perform on both days.
The performance will last until 21:15pm. Although there is a certain distance between the event venue and the dormitory area, each student has a different level of acceptance of sound, so we apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.

In order to accommodate this large-scale event, some of the spaces and parking lots on campus will be controlled as follows: 
Library: Closed from 10th May (Sat) to 11th May (Sun). For self-study, students may go to the K Book Centre or C109 classroom of the School of Management.
Car Parking: The car park of the Administration Building and the temporary car park of the Administration Building will be under site control from 00:00 on 10 May to 24:00 on 11 May. Students who need to move their cars are requested to do so before 23:59 on 9 May to move their cars to other places such as Renichi Car Park and Qianmen Motorcycle Parking Lot.

We apologise for any inconvenience caused and thank you for your cooperation and support!
