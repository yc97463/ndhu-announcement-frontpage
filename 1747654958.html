【國立東華大學113學年度畢業典禮】敬邀出席🎉 [National Dong Hwa University 2025 Graduation Ceremony] Invitation to attend🎉

2025/05/19 19:42 ·
活動公告 ·
學務處畢業生及校友服務組 info 
各位親愛的師長您好：
        國立東華大學113學年度畢業典禮將於2025/05/24(週六)舉行🎊，誠摯的邀請各位師長一同至會場（體育館）給予畢業生們祝福。
        敬邀各位師長於當日下午15：20前抵達集合點(活動中心鏡廳)，在校長與副校長的陪伴下，帶領畢業生緩緩進入會場；並請您在抵達「師長祝福區」時暫停您的腳步，將畢業生送入會場後，再由校長及副校長在畢業生的歡迎下，帶領各位師長一同入席。
💥【113學年度畢業典禮】專屬網頁訊息已放置於本校網站首頁，
內含畢業典禮所有相關資訊，歡迎查詢。
活動網址：http://reurl.cc/4Lr1oK
學生事務處  敬邀

Dear Respected Faculty,
   The 2025 Graduation Ceremony of National Dong Hwa University will be held on Saturday, May 24, 2025. 🎊,
We sincerely invite you to attend the ceremony at the Gymnasium and offer your blessings to our graduating students on this momentous occasion.
   All faculty members are kindly asked to arrive at the assembly point (Mirror Hall, Student Activity Center) by 3:20 PM.
    Accompanied by the President and Vice President, you will walk together with the graduates into the venue. Upon reaching the “Faculty Blessing Area”, please pause briefly to send your regards to the graduates. After all graduates have entered the hall, the President and Vice President will lead the faculty into the seating area amid the graduates’ warm welcome.

💥[2025 Graduation Ceremony]
Exclusive webpage information has been placed on the homepage of the school website. The website contains all relevant information about the graduation ceremony, welcome to inquire.
Event website: http://reurl.cc/4Lr1oK
