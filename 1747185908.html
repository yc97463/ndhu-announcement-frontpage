國立東華大學公告

本校教學區將於114年5月22日至5月23日進行病媒蚊防治消毒作業，敬請配合。Mosquito-Borne Disease Prevention and Disinfection in the Academic Area on May 22nd and 23rd, 2025

2025/05/14 09:25 ·
行政公告 ·
總務處環境保護組 info 
一、為配合畢業典禮相關活動，本校預定於114年5月22日（星期四）至5月23日（星期五）針對教學區建築物室外進行病媒蚊防治消毒工作。
二、消毒日期及範圍依下列順序進行：
(一) 施作日期：114年5月22日（星期四）至5月23日（星期五）
(二)施作範圍:
1.行政大樓
2.藝術學院
3.人文社會學院(1、2、3館)
4.教育學院
5.管理學院
6.學生活動中心
7.圖書資訊大樓
8.理工學院(1、2、3館)
9.育成中心
10.湖畔餐廳周邊
11.原住民民族學院
12.環境解說中心
13.環境學院
14.體育館
三、消毒期間，請勿靠近施作區域，並請協助關閉各棟一、二樓面向室外的門窗。消毒作業期間造成不便，敬請見諒。
總務處環境保護組 敬啟
I. In response to the upcoming graduation ceremony activities, our school is scheduled to conduct outdoor disinfection work for mosquito-borne disease prevention in the academic area buildings from May 22nd to May 23rd, 2025.
II. The disinfection dates and locations will be carried out in the following order:
(I) Implementation Dates: May 22nd (Thursday) to May 23rd (Friday), 2025
(II) Scope of Work (in order):
1. Administration Building
2. College of Arts
3. College of Humanities and Social Sciences (Buildings 1, 2, and 3)
4. College of Education
5. College of Management
6. Student Activity Center
7. Library and Information Building
8. College of Science and Engineering (Buildings 1, 2, and 3)
9. Incubation Center
10. Lakeside Restaurant Area
11. College of Indigenous Studies
12. Environmental Education Center
13. College of Environmental Studies
14.Indoor Sports Center
III. During the disinfection period, please do not approach the work areas and kindly cooperate by closing all outward-facing doors andwindows on the first and second floors. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
Office of General Affairs Environmental Protection Division NDHU
