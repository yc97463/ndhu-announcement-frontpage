【通識】轉知~114年度全國夏季學院開課囉!今年暑假一起來上跨校通識抵學分！(4/23(三)開始報名)

2025/04/23 08:48 ·
課程公告 ·
通識教育中心 info 
親愛的同學您好：

暑假想來點不一樣的通識課嗎？114年度暑期「全國夏季學院」推出全臺27所大學所開設的43門精選通識課程，不只讓你取得學分，還讓你眼界大開。 

臺東大學今年特別開設推出部落遊學系列課程，邀你走入原鄉、住進臺東，體驗最真實的南島文化，「南島文化概論」走訪卑南族部落；「原住民族部落學習與體驗」前進蘭嶼深入達悟族、排灣族部落等，親身感受土地與文化魅力，並提供在臺東大學住宿的名額。

臺灣大學所開設之「災害風險管理」包含防災士培訓並與慈濟基金會合作，從災害情境模擬中學會冷靜應對，親手搭設福慧床、隔屏，學會安置技術等親身體驗，挑戰你對「防災」的認知，提高災時生存機率！

修課不只是為了學分，而是為了讓自己在真實世界中，站得更穩。第一階段有最多的選課名額，快來看看有什麼課程，加入我們一起探索學習的無限可能！

Dear Students,

Looking for something different this summer?

2025 Summer College is offering 43 carefully curated general education courses across 27 universities throughout Taiwan that not only help you earn credits but also broaden your horizons.

National Taitung University presents a special Tribal Study Tour Series, inviting you to step into Indigenous communities, stay in Taitung, and immerse yourself in authentic Austronesian cultures. In "Introduction to Austronesian Cultures" you'll visit the Puyuma tribe; while"Indigenous tribe learning and experience" takes you deep into the world of the Tao and Paiwan tribes on Lanyu Island. Accommodation at National Taitung University is available for enrolled students.

"Disaster Risk Management" course includes disaster prevention specialist training in collaboration with Tzu Chi Foundation. You'll simulate disaster scenarios, learn to respond with calm, and get hands-on experience assembling Tzu Chi's foldable beds and partitions—equipping you with essential emergency sheltering techniques.This course will challenge your understanding of disaster preparedness and boost your survival chances during real emergencies.

Three of the courses are conducted entirely in English: Application and Health Function of Spices and Herbs (distance learning, synchronous), Fundamentals of English Writing (in-person instruction), and General Psychology (in-person instruction).

Taking a course isn't just about earning credits—it's about becoming stronger in the real world.The first course selection phase offers the most available seats. Don't miss out—check out the courses and join us in exploring the endless possibilities of learning!
