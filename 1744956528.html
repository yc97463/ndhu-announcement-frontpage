【活動公告】113學年度第2學期『與校長有約』座談會【Event Announcement】Welcome to join “Meeting with the President”

2025/04/18 14:08 ·
活動公告 ·
學務處生活輔導組 info 
親愛的同學:
校長誠摯邀請您2025/5/16(五)前來參加「與校長有約」座談會，希望透過輕鬆的方式與您面對面溝通，了解您的想法與需求，並說明學校推動重大政策實施目的及近期推動的事項。
歡迎提出問題或建議，可下載提問單、填寫後，寄至 活動聯絡人王先生信箱 rlawwh@gms.ndhu.edu.tw，以利彙整。
當日提供餐點，為利統計人數，敬請事先報名。(https://osa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/423-1005-3507.php)
	
活動時間：2025/5/16(五)12:30~14:00
活動地點：理工一館 第一講堂A108
提問單受理：2025/4/21(一) ～ 2025/4/30(三)
報名受理：2025/4/23(三) ~2025/5/13(二)
 因為當日會場為室內空間，請自備口罩。

活動聯絡人：生活輔導組王先生  03-890-6221

您參與，學務處開心

學務處生活輔導組
 =========================================================
Dear fellow students,
“Meeting with the President” will be held on 2025/5/16(Fri).  President Chao will interact with students face-to-face, to further understand what students think and need.  On this occasion, the President will also brief upcoming events and explain purposes of important school policies.
Students are welcome to offer any suggestion or inquiry beforehand to Mr. Wang (rlawwh@gms.ndhu.edu.tw).

Free lunch would be cordially provided for students who have signed up in advance.  
(For environmental protection, please bring your own tableware.)

(https://osa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/423-1005-3507.php)

Date: 2025/5/16(Fri) 12:30 - 14:00
Place: Room A108, Science and Engineering Building I
Inquiry submission: 2025/4/21(Mon) ~ 2025/4/30(Wed)
Sign-up: 2025/4/23(Wed) ～2025/5/13(Tue)
Because the venue is an indoor space, please bring your own mask.
