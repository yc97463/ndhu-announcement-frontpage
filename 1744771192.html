親愛的朋友們，

花蓮縣為辦理「2026年國際少年運動會(ICG) 」及「世界俱樂部龍舟錦標賽 (CCWC)」，委請東華大學語言中心負責培訓英語服務人員 ，故本中心著手儲訓人員招募，邀請對此次國際賽事有興趣的朋友一起共襄盛舉，只要您
1. 已滿18歲 (【希望工程】回鄉服務學生除外)及
2. 英語能力達B1以上就可參加嘍!

英語服務人員必須有足夠英文溝通能力，與國際運動員、教練及賽事相關人員交流，藉由服務學習成長，讓自己的學習履歷更亮眼，目前正在進行第一階段的服務人員招募及儲訓，待通過2個整天儲訓課程後，花蓮縣政府將發予儲訓結業證書，正式成為英語服務培訓人員 (第一階段)，實習 (第二階段) 及正式培訓課程 (第三階段) 將於儲訓後公告。

Dear Friends,

To successfully host the "2026 International Children's Games (ICG)" and "Club Crew World Championships (CCWC)," the Hualien County Government has commissioned the Language Center of National Dong Hwa University to organize language helper training. Our center is now recruiting and preparing staff for both events. We warmly invite anyone interested in these international opportunities to join us!

You are eligible to apply if you are
1. at least 18 years old (except for students participating in the "Hualien Education Hope Project") and
2. have an English proficiency of B1 level or above!

English helpers must demonstrate strong communication skills in English to interact with international athletes, coaches, and other event personnel. This service-learning opportunity will allow you to enhance your personal growth and strengthen your academic and professional portfolio.

We are currently recruiting preparatory English helpers (phase 1). Once participants complete a two-day training cycle, they will receive a certificate of completion issued by the Hualien County Government and officially become trained preparatory English helpers. The schedule for internship (phase 2) and formal training (phase 3) sessions will be announced after the preparation stage.
