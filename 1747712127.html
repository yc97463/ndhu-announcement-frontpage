🗳️🍦【民主冰棒，因為你的選擇值得一支！】🍦🗳️

你知道嗎？在遙遠的澳洲，選舉日有個可愛又特別的傳統——「民主香腸」（Democratic Sausage）！大家在投票所投下神聖的一票後，會習慣買根香腸邊吃邊聊，這根「民主香腸」就成了他們公民參與、凝聚社群力量的獨特象徵。

現在，東華大學也要創造屬於我們的民主新文化！

第15屆學生自治會選舉，即將在5/22(四)進行投票。今年，我們不只要你投票，更想讓你感受到，你的每一票，真的很重要！

不管你支持誰，不管你怎麼選，
只要你有行動，我們請你吃冰！

📱 拿出手機，出示「完成投票」的畫面
🎁 即可當場領取「民主冰棒」一支！
⏳ 上、下午各發放500支冰棒，合計1000支，數量有限，發完為止！

民主不該只是口號，
讓我們用一支冰棒，感謝你對校園的認真參與。

📍 領取地點｜湖畔餐廳外廣場
🕒 時間｜5/22(四) 9:00~17:00
🔗 投票方式｜Zuvio 線上投票

🗳️🍦Democracy Ice Pop – Because Your Choice Deserves a Treat!🍦🗳️

Did you know? In Australia, there's a fun election day tradition called the "Democracy Sausage"! After voting, people enjoy a sausage while chatting with others. It’s a tasty way to celebrate civic participation and community spirit.

Now, NDHU is starting our own voting tradition!

The 15th Student Association Election is coming on Thursday, May 22. This year, we want you to feel that your vote truly matters!

No matter who you vote for,
No matter how you vote,
As long as you vote - we'll give you a free ice pop! 🎉

📱 Show your "voted" screen on your phone
🎁 And get one Democracy Ice Pop!
⏳ 500 ice pops in the morning and 500 in the afternoon - 1,000 in total, while supplies last!

Let's make democracy more than just a word.
Let's celebrate it with an ice pop!
