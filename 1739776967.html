〈國立東華大學學期16週調整意向調查〉
各位同學好：
近年來，國內許多大學已陸續將學期長度調整為 16 週，以提升學習彈性並與國際接軌。日前本校行政會議已初步討論此議題，然而，學期調整涉及課程安排、學習品質、學生權益等多方面影響，因此最終決策仍需廣納學生的意見，同時考量到現行《大學法施行細則》仍規定每學分應修滿18小時為原則，若實施16週應提出相應之配套措施，及完成校內共識及報教育部同意等程序。
本問卷旨在了解同學對一學期16週的看法，包括可能的優勢、潛在影響及需求，以確保學校政策的制定能符合多數師生的期待與學習環境需求。你的意見將成為學生會向學校反映的重要依據，影響未來學期制度的調整方向。
我們誠摯邀請每一位東華學生參與本問卷，表達你的看法，讓學校決策更貼近學生的需求！

🕒 填答時間：約 3-5 分鐘
📆 問卷截止日：2025 年 2月 24 日
📍 問卷為匿名調查，僅限學校帳號填寫一次，結果開放即時查看
🔗  問卷連結：https://forms.gle/TMazvvcTWHcAGCYS7

〈National Dong Hwa University Semester Adjustment Survey: 16-Week Semester〉
Dear Students,
In recent years, many universities in Taiwan have adjusted their semester length to 16 weeks to enhance learning flexibility and align with international standards. A preliminary discussion on this topic has been held at our university's administrative meeting. However, adjusting the semester length involves various aspects, such as course scheduling, learning quality, and student rights. Therefore, the final decision requires comprehensive student feedback. Moreover, according to the current Enforcement Rules of the University Act, each credit hour must consist of 18 hours. If a 16-week semester is implemented, corresponding supporting measures must be proposed, and internal consensus must be reached, followed by approval from the Ministry of Education.
This survey aims to gather students' opinions on a 16-week semester, including its potential advantages, impacts, and needs. Your feedback will serve as a crucial reference for the Student Association in communicating with the university and influencing the future direction of semester policies.
We sincerely invite every Dong Hwa student to participate in this survey and share your views to help the university make decisions that better meet student needs!

🕒 Estimated Time to Complete: 3–5 minutes
📆 Survey Deadline: February 24, 2025
📍 This is an anonymous survey. Each student can submit the survey once using their university account. Real-time results will be available.
