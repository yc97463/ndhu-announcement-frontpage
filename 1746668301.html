教務處註冊組公告114-1轉系所申請注意事項如下

轉系所申請作業為線上審核(無須列印紙本申請書)
一、第一志願轉系、所、學位學程審理期間
(一)學生提出下一學期轉系、所、學位學程申請：114/5/19-6/13
(二)不需要看本學期成績的系、所、學位學程審理期間：114/5/19-7/7(僅生醫系、物理系、歷史系、臺灣系、族文系)
(三)需要看含本學期成績的系、所、學位學程審理期間：114/7/1-7/7
(四)若第一志願系、所、學位學程不同意，改送第二志願系、所、學位學程審理期間：114/7/8-7/10
All application forms will be reviewed online. After the initial department reviews the application form, the form will forward to the first-choice department/institute and college to review ( Transcript not required : May 19th to July 7th, 2025; Transcript required: July 1st to July 7th, 2025). Once the first-choice department rejects the application, the form will deliver to the second-choice department/institute and college to review (July 8th to July 10th, 2024).
二、申請流程
(一) 轉系申請作業均為線上申請及線上審核，請學生於114/5/19-6/13至東華大學首頁/行政單位/教務處/教務資訊系統/註冊/其他/「學生轉系所申請系統」( https://sys.ndhu.edu.tw/AA/REG/TransferDep/student/login.aspx )中填妥擬申請轉系所資料，並上傳第一、二志願系所要求的備審資料PDF檔(一個檔案上限2M)，即完成申請程序(無須列印紙本申請書)；請學生務必主動與班級導師約談，另系統會同步以郵件通知班級導師與學生進行輔導約談。
Transfer applications will be reviewed online only. Application is open from May 19th to June 13th, 2025. Please submit the application form and upload documents (PDF file only with a maximum of 2M) to " the Student Transfer Application System"( https://sys.ndhu.edu.tw/AA/REG/TransferDep/student/login.aspx ). Once submitted successfully, please make sure to consult with the applicant’s class advisor by yourself, the system will email the applicant’s advisor to consult with the applicant.
(二)受理轉系的系所若需查看學生成績科目時，可在「學生轉系申請系統」中查詢(學生無須提供紙本成績單)，配合113-2學期教師上網登錄成績於114/6/27結束，教務處註冊組預計於114/6/30下班前公告開放網路成績查詢。
The prospective department can check applicants’ transcripts from " the Student Transfer Application System". Due to the Spring 2025 (113-2) teacher’s online login results will be ending by June 27th, 2025, it is expected that the Registry Division of the Office of Academic Affairs will announce this semester grades after June 30th, 2025.
(三)等第一、二志願系所線上審理完成後，教務處註冊組將下載核准轉系所名冊依行政程序陳判至校長同意後，預計於114/7/25前於教務處網頁/公告事項中公告(僅公告同意轉系名單，不同意部分將不會公告) 。
The Registry Division of the Office of Academic Affairs will announce the result before July 25th, 2025.
(四)學生轉系所係以一次為原則。
In principle, students can only apply for transfer applications once.
(五)請同學依下載的學雜費繳費單繳費，日後出納組會請學生多退少補。
Please pay the tuition fees according to the downloaded payment slip. The Cashier Division will ask students to refund the excess and make up for the deficiency in the future.
(六)獲准轉系學生若有選課問題請洽教務處課務組詢問。
Approved transfer applications students who have course selection issues should contact the Curriculum Division of the Office of Academic Affairs for inquiries.
(七)獲准轉系學生請自行決定補發新系所的悠遊學生證(一張220元) 。
Students who have been approved to transfer applications are required to decide whether to apply for a new EasyCard for the new department ( NT$220 per card).

P.S.若學士班學生已上傳畢業課規年，自教務處註冊組於「學籍管理系統」中設定轉系後(114.8.5前)，將自動取消學生原上傳課規年的設定，請學士班獲准轉系的學生重新至「學士班學程選課輔助及畢業初審系統」( https://web.ndhu.edu.tw/aa/graduate/Login.aspx )上傳新學系之畢業課規年度，以利畢業初審。
The Registry Division of the Office of Academic Affairs anticipate that they will complete registration of transfers in the student status management system before August 5th,2025.
After August 5th,2025, students who have been approved to transfer applications are required to re-upload the academic year of the bachelor's degree course planning for the new department by “the Smart Assistant for Course Planning and Initial Assessment for Graduation”( https://web.ndhu.edu.tw/aa/graduate/Login.aspx ).
