學務處畢業生及校友服務組 info 
明日5/24（六）是本校的大日子，校內車流量不少，
請各位要注意校內交通管制路線，路線圖如附件1，

📣畢業生集合啦~
請各位畢業生一定要穿著學士服才能入場!!!!
請各院畢業生在下午15點20分前集合於理工二館及學生活動中心如附件2至3；
學務處有安排舉牌小天使😇在此等候各位~不見不散~
（若下雨會另外通知各位）
畢業生進會場時，依舊會有小天使帶隊，但還是怕有沒跟上的畢業生，
所以咱們附上會場位置圖，脫隊的你就可以自行入座啦~
👉️如果沒有報名，突然想參加，我們也是很歡迎，
但請往預備的座位入座，避免影響他人哦!

Tomorrow, 5/24 (Saturday), is a big day for our school. There will be a lot of traffic on campus.
Please pay attention to the traffic control routes on campus. The route map is attached.

📣 Hey Graduates, it’s time to gather~
All graduates, please be sure to wear your Graduation gown before entering the venue!!!!
Please gather by 3:20 PM at Science and Engineering Building 2 and the Student Activity Center, as shown in the attached picture.
Office of Student Affair has arranged little angels holding signs to guide you~ 😇 
(You will be notified if it rains.)
When graduates enter the venue, there will still be little angels leading the group, but we are still afraid that there will be graduates who fail to follow, so we have attached a map of the location of the venue, so if you fall out of the group, you can still find your seat~
👉️If you haven’t registered and suddenly want to participate, we are very welcome, but please go to the reserved seats to avoid from disturbing others!
