📣當天 18:30～18:50 將安排音樂會前的導聆，由音樂系爵士組的兼任講師，小提琴家江忻薇老師，帶給大家愉快且能聽懂、聽好的豐收夜晚！

來自日本的國際知名小號手、現任洗足學園音樂大學教授——  #原朋直（Hara Tomonao），將與同為小號手的藝文中心主任 #魏廣晧，攜手金曲獎鋼琴家 #許郁瑛、日本低音提琴手 #山田洋平，以及法德混血爵士鼓手 #Gabriel Hahn，組成重磅五重奏，為東華帶來一場不容錯過的音樂盛宴。

原朋直以情感飽滿的溫暖音色聞名，不僅活躍於爵士樂界，更因電視節目《情熱大陸》介紹而走紅，參與北野武電影《四海兄弟》與NHK-BS《World News》主題曲錄製，亦曾擔任Yamaha廣告的演出與編曲。
而他所任教的洗足學園音樂大學，更是日本第一個開設爵士樂主修的音樂高等學府，孕育知名音樂人，如平原綾香、小林香織。

 誠邀老牌樂迷或初探爵士的你，
 在初夏微熱的夜，
 隨著悠揚的雙小號，潛入爵士的自由海洋。

---------
An internationally renowned trumpeter of Japan and current professor at Senzoku Gakuen College of Music, Hara Tomonao, will join forces with fellow trumpeter and the chairperson of NDHU Arts Center, Stacey Wei, Golden Melody-winning pianist, Yuying Hsu, Japanese jazz Bassist, Yohei Yamada, and France-German jazz drummer, Gabriel Hahn to form a powerhouse quintet. Together, they will bring an unmissable musical feast to NDHU.

Hara Tomonao is renowned for his warm, emotionally rich trumpet tone. Active not only in the jazz scene, he rose to broader fame after being featured on the TV program Jounetsu Tairiku. He has contributed to the soundtrack of Takeshi Kitano’s film Brothers, recorded the theme song for NHK-BS’s World News, and has also performed and arranged music for Yamaha commercials.

Additionally, Senzoku Gakuen College of Music, where he currently teaches, is the first higher education institution to offer a major in jazz in Japan. It has nurtured many renowned musicians, including Ayaka Hirahara and Kaori Kobayashi.

A sincere invitation to both longtime jazz enthusiasts and those new to the genre,
on a gently warm early summer night,
let the mallow sound of dual trumpets guide you into the boundless sea of jazz.
