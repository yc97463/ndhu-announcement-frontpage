113-2 Teaching Survey - Prize Winner Announcement

獲獎同學請於即日起至6月25日(三) 8:30-17:00，攜帶「學生證」至行政大樓5樓502室-課務組領獎，逾期視同自願放棄獲獎資格。

Winner please bring your student card to Curriulum Division (Administration Building 502) before June 25 (8:30 am-5 pm) to claim your gift card! Failure to respond by the deadline will be deemed a voluntary forfeiture of the award eligibility.

※備註:委託代領者，需備妥「獲獎人」及「代領人」之學生證，並協助確認獲獎人相關個人資訊（身分證字號、戶籍地址）是否正確。
