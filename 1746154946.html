Let's weave together and feel the culture!
想親手編織屬於你的原住民風格小物嗎？
這次我們邀請你一起動手體驗阿美族傳統編織工藝，
跟著老師一步步穿針引線，感受線與文化的溫度！
This is a great chance to learn about Taiwan's Indigenous culture through a fun DIY weaving workshop!
Come join us and create your own beautiful piece while experiencing the spirit of the Amis people.
