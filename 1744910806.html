沒有樂器，還能如何演繹音樂？
只有人聲，如何打造衝擊感官的現場表演？
 
4月23日，我們把教科書等級的韓國阿卡貝拉天團帶來東華了——
 
Zenith，意即「#人聲是最好的樂器」，成立於2008年，曾於2015年奧地利格拉茨「vokal.total國際阿卡貝拉大賽」及2014年TCMC台灣國際重唱藝術節「世界盃現代阿卡貝拉大賽」榮獲大獎，是 #首支於國際大賽中獲獎的韓國隊伍。
 
從多變的聲音表現、合作無間的表演默契到生動的肢體語言，五人編制的Zenith將為我們帶來超乎想像的視覺聽覺雙重饗宴✨
 
座位有限，記得提早到場，在春天的尾聲💚擁抱音樂💚🎶
（2019年，Zenith被編進韓國小學的現代阿卡貝拉教學範例，是貨真價實的 #教科書等級🤘）

Without instruments, how can music be interpreted?
With only the human voice, how can a performance deliver a powerful sensory impact?

On April 23rd, we're bringing a top-tier Korean a cappella group, the kind you read about in textbooks, to NDHU!

Zenith, which means “the human voice is the best instrument”, was founded in 2008. Having won top prizes at the “vokal.total International A Cappella Competition” in Graz, Austria in 2015, and at the “World Contemporary A Cappella Competition” held by TCMC Taiwan International Contemporary A Cappella Festival in 2014, Zenith became the #first Korean group to win awards in international competitions.

From diverse vocal performances and flawless teamwork to expressive body language, the 5-member group Zenith is set to deliver an audiovisual feast beyond imagination.✨

Seats are limited, so we welcome everyone to arrive early. Let us 💚
embrace the music 💚🎶 at the end of spring.
(In 2019, Zenith was featured as a contemporary a cappella teaching example in South Korean elementary school textbooks — truly a #textbook-level group.🤘)
