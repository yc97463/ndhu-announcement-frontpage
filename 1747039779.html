【國立東華大學第15屆學生自治會選舉 選舉公報】
熱騰騰的選舉公報上線啦！本次選舉共有3組正副會長、18名議員參選，透過選舉公報可以了解候選人的基本資料、經歷及政見，幫助選民更加能決定如何投入手中神聖的一票。

電子版選舉公報可參考附件，或至選委會網站閱覽：
https://dhsa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/406-1110-241068.php
實體版選舉公報近期將陸續張貼於各學院公布欄。
＊＊＊
🎤重要事項宣導：
●候選人政見辯論發表會
時間：5/14(三) 18:30~21:00
地點：管理學院第二講堂

線上直播：本會YouTube頻道（https://www.youtube.com/@%E6%9D%B1%E8%8F%AF%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%B8%E9%81%B8%E5%A7%94%E6%9C%83）
歡迎各位同學踴躍參與，現場將有選民提問環節，實體到場和線上收看直播的同學都可以向本屆選舉正副會長候選人發問喔!!

●選舉線上投票
時間：5/22(四) 9:00~17:00
地點：Zuvio線上投票
電腦、手機、APP皆可投，用你手中的選票，投向東華的未來!

【15th Student Association Election Bulletin – National Dong Hwa University】
The new election bulletin is now available!
This year, there are 3 teams running for President and Vice President of the Student Association, and 18 students running for parliamentarians.
The bulletin has each candidate’s basic info, experience, and policy proposals, so you can learn more and decide who to vote for.
📄 Online election bulletin: Check the attachment or visit the Election Commission website:
https://dhsa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/406-1110-241068.php
📌 Paper version: Will be posted soon on bulletin boards around each college.
***
🎤 Important Events:
● Candidate Debate
🕕 Time: Wednesday, May 14, 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM
📍 Place: Lecture  Theater 2 (D114 Room), College of Management
▶️ Live stream: Our YouTube channel
https://www.youtube.com/@%E6%9D%B1%E8%8F%AF%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%B8%E9%81%B8%E5%A7%94%E6%9C%83
All students are welcome to join! You can ask questions to the candidates during the event or in the live chat.
● Online Voting
🗓 Date: Thursday, May 22, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
📍 Place: Zuvio online voting system:  https://irs.zuvio.com.tw/
You can vote using a computer, mobile, or the app.
Use your vote to support the future of Dong Hwa!
