嘿，一起來跳舞啊！

我們邀請你，在4月18日晚間七點前來到湖畔廣場，一起在爵士樂的節奏中搖擺起來！由東華音樂系爵士組現場演奏，伴著星空和微風，讓身體自由律動。你只需要帶上一雙方便活動的鞋子，和一顆想跳舞的心😍
搖擺舞（Swing Dance）是什麼？ 它起源於20世紀初的美國，是一種節奏明快、充滿活力的社交舞蹈。隨著爵士樂的興起，搖擺舞也成為當時最流行的舞風。搖擺舞不講究標準舞步，重點在於和舞伴的互動與節奏感，所以就算你完全沒跳過舞，也能輕鬆上手，享受和人一起動起來的快樂！
這次我們特別邀請了全台灣第一支搖擺舞團「Naughty Swing搖擺舞團」帶來教學。他們融合街舞背景，經常活躍於國內外搖擺舞比賽，甚至在2018及2022年拿下世界搖擺舞大賽冠軍！ 如此厲害的舞團，不管有沒有跳過舞，都歡迎大家拋開期中考煩惱，一起來搖擺吧！

Hey, let’s dance together!

We would like to invite you to come to the Lakeside Plaza at 19:00 on Apr. 18, and swing to the rhythm of jazz music with us! With a live performance by the NDHU Jazz Band, accompanied by the starry sky and a gentle breeze, allowing our body to move freely to the rhythm. What you need to bring are just a pair of comfortable shoes for movement, and a ready-to-dance heart.

What is Swing Dance? It originated in the early 20th century in the US, and is a lively and rhythmically upbeat social dance. Alone with the rise of jazz music, swing dance became the most popular dance style at that time. There are no standard steps in swing dance, the focus is on the interaction and the sense of rhythm with your partner. So even if you've never danced before, you can easily get the hang of it and enjoy the joy of moving with others!

This time, we especially invited the first swing dance group in Taiwan, Naughty Swing, to provide the teaching. They combine the background with street dance, and are often active in swing dance competitions both domestically and internationally, even winning the championships of the world swing dance competition in 2018 and 2022! Such an amazing dance group! Whether you've danced before, everyone is welcome to shake off the stress of midterms and swing with us together!
