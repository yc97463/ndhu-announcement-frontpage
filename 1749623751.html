【2025 你丟我撿 免費市集】
畢業季限定-24小時開放！
畢業了？搬宿舍？斷捨離？
你是不是也有這些煩惱：
 東西太多，搬不走？
 還能用但沒人接收？
 打掃清出一堆雜物？
 歐趴後再也不想看到課本？
讓好物被看見、被需要、被延續
拿來「你丟我撿」免費市集！
舊物變新寶，撿寶也自由！
活動資訊
時間- 5/23（五）～ 6/16（一)
地點- 集賢館 B1 學生會會辦旁
方式- 24 小時開放，留給他人的就放下，喜歡的就帶走
以下類別不收：
✘ 食物／食品容器
✘ 衣服、鞋子、內衣褲
✘ 牙刷、口罩、化妝工具等衛生用品
✘ 醫療用品
✘ 寢具類（枕頭、棉被）
✘ 已開封液體清潔品（洗髮精、乳液等）
✘ 垃圾、壞掉的物品 一律不收
小提醒：
現場沒有人力管理，路過時歡迎順手整理一下！不確定能否擺放的物品，請私訊粉專提前詢問！

【2025 Free Exchange Market】
Graduation Season Special | Open 24 Hours!
Graduating? Moving out of your dorm? Decluttering?
Are you dealing with these struggles?
 Too much stuff to take with you?
 Usable items but no one to give them to?
 Found a pile of random things while cleaning?
 Never want to see your textbooks again after finals?
Let your pre-loved items be seen, needed, and given a second life!
Bring them to our “Your Discard, My Treasure” Free Market —
where old things become new treasures, and treasure-hunting is totally free!
Event Details
Date: May 23 (Fri) – June 16 (Mon)
Location: Jixian Building B1, next to the Student Association Office
Format: Open 24 hours. Leave what you no longer need, take what you like!
Items we do not accept:
✘ Food or food containers
✘ Clothing, shoes, underwear
✘ Toothbrushes, masks, makeup tools, or other hygiene items
✘ Medical supplies
✘ Bedding (pillows, blankets)
✘ Opened liquid products (e.g., shampoo, lotion)
✘ Trash or broken items — strictly not accepted
Friendly reminder:
There is no staff managing the site, so feel free to help keep it tidy when you pass by!
Not sure if something is suitable to leave? Message our page in advance to ask!
