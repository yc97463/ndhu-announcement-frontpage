[演講公告]歐洲議會資訊處處長Aleliunaite女士蒞校分享：歐盟的歷史與組織

2025/02/26 16:48 ·
研討會 ·
朱副校長室 info 
歐盟研究中心邀請歐洲議會資訊處處長Aleliunaite女士，蒞臨東華大學與師生們分享歐盟的歷史與機構發展。Aleliunaite女士負責歐洲議會的數位資訊與資安工作，並曾深入參與歐盟數位治理的關鍵法規，包括在國際社會中廣受關注的歐盟一般資料保護法（GDPR）與歐盟人工智能法（EU AI Act）的制定。Aleliunaite女士將結合其在數位治理領域的深厚專業知識，分享歐盟如何在不斷變動的全球數位時代中，發展出具影響力的制度體系，並解釋歐盟如何藉由強化數位治理來應對現代挑戰與機遇。

此場講座不僅提供深入了解歐盟歷史與機構運作的難得機會，更是一次探索歐盟在數位時代如何塑造全球治理架構的重要時刻。我們誠摯邀請對歐盟、數位治理及未來發展有興趣的師生，參與這場充滿啟發與對話的學術盛會，與Aleliunaite處長一同交流，共同探討歐盟如何面對全球化與數位化的雙重挑戰。

The EU Research Center invites Ms. Aleliunaite, Director of the Information Office at the European Parliament, to visit National Dong Hwa University and share insights on the history and institutional development of the European Union. Ms. Aleliunaite is responsible for digital information and cybersecurity at the European Parliament and has been deeply involved in the development of key EU digital governance regulations, including the widely discussed General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act). Drawing on her extensive expertise in digital governance, she will explain how the EU has developed an influential institutional framework to navigate the rapidly changing global digital landscape and how it strengthens digital governance to address modern challenges and opportunities.

This lecture offers a unique opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of the history and institutional workings of the EU, while also providing valuable insights into how the EU is shaping the global governance framework in the digital age. We warmly invite faculty and students interested in the EU, digital governance, and future development to join this inspiring and interactive academic event, engaging in a dialogue with Director Aleliunaite to explore how the EU is addressing the dual challenges of globalization and digitalization.
