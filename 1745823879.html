🔥113學年度畢業典禮開放報名至5月5日（一）下午17點整，畢業祝福好禮等你哦！🎉

2025/04/28 15:04 ·
活動公告 ·
學務處畢業生及校友服務組 info 
親愛的應屆畢業生，您們好：
✨5月24日(六)是本校畢業典禮，這是一個非常重要且值得慶祝🎉的日子。
當日下午16時將於本校體育館，舉辦全校畢業典禮儀式，我們誠摯地邀請您一同共襄盛舉。
本屆畢業典禮除了隆重的授證代表儀式，校長還為本屆畢業生送上優厚的祝福禮抽獎活動，敬請期待精美好禮。

🎯畢業生們請立馬手刀報名:

Dear Graduating Students
  We are delighted to announce that the school graduation ceremony will be held on Saturday, May 24, 2025, at 4 pm in the gymnasium. We sincerely invite you to join us in celebrating this momentous occasion. 🎉
 In addition to the conferral of degrees, our president will warmly host a special lucky draw, featuring thoughtfully prepared graduation gifts for this year's graduates. Please join us for this wonderful event!

🎯Register now through the link below to participate and have a chance to win prizes: 
https://sys.ndhu.edu.tw/SA/XSL_ApplyRWD/ActAnnounce.aspx?ActID=24843
❤️Friendly reminder: This lucky draw is exclusively for 2025 graduating students who register in advance and attend the school-wide ceremony in person.  The registration deadline is 5 pm, May 5, 2025.
📌 For entry guidelines and dress code details, please visit the link below.
    https://osa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/404-1005-238742.php?Lang=zh-tw

We look forward to celebrating with you.
