誰是謝明諺？

或許你對他的名字不熟悉，但他的音樂可能早已觸動你的耳朵。他是台灣當今頂尖的薩克斯風手之一，榮獲了第三十屆金曲獎《演奏類最佳專輯獎》，也致力於推廣爵士樂文化。謝明諺在2022年擔任兩廳院夏日爵士戶外派對的音樂總監，引領著台灣爵士樂的前進，而這次，他將帶來與日本爵士樂碰撞的火花！ 

Suga Dairo new little one 是由日本即興鋼琴的代表人物スガダイロー（Suga Dairo）領軍，與在東京極為活躍的年輕吉他手細井德太郎和鼓手秋元修所組成的三重奏—new little one。他們曾受謝明諺之邀一起參加台中爵士音樂節，並合作發行了四人的首張錄音室專輯，在日本與台灣皆大受好評，更榮獲第十五屆金音創作獎「最佳爵士歌曲獎」。 吉他手細井德太郎持續著各種藝術跨界的演出，並組了一個名為「細井徳太郎とタコ⾜イヤホンズ」的樂團；鼓手秋元修更是以第一名在洗足音樂大學爵士系畢業，隨後開始在各個國家演出。 

每一位樂手各有千秋，當他們聚集在一起時會產生什麼化學反應呢？就讓我們一起期待吧！
2月24日在東華大學藝術學院音樂廳，誠邀您一起參與並感受爵士樂的美妙之夜。 

Who is Minyen Hsieh?

Perhaps you are not familiar with his name, but his music has probably touched your ears. He is one of Taiwan’s top saxophonists today, having won the “Best Instrumental Album Award” of the 30th Golden Melody Awards. He also dedicates himself to promoting jazz music culture. Minyen Hsieh served as the music director for the 2022 National Theater and Concert Hall Summer Jazz Outdoor Party, leading the advancement of jazz music in Taiwan. This time, he will bring sparks of collision with Japanese jazz!

Suga Dairo new little one is a trio led by Suga Dairo, a prominent figure in Japanese improvised piano music, with young guitarist Hosoi Tokutaro and drummer Akimoto Shu, both of whom are highly active in Tokyo. They were once invited by Minyen Hsieh to participate in the Taichung Jazz Festival, and collaborated on releasing their first studio album as a quartet. The album is widely acclaimed in both Japan and Taiwan, and even won the “Best Jazz Song Award” at the 15th Golden Indie Music Awards. The guitarist Hosoi Tokutaro continues to engage in various cross-disciplinary artistic performances and has formed a band called “細井徳太郎とタコ⾜イヤホンズ”. The drummer Akimoto Shu graduated as the top student from the Jazz Department of Senzoku Gakuen College of Music, he then embarked on a journey akin to traveling the world, performing in various countries.

Each musician has their own strengths. What kind of chemical reaction will occur when they come together? Let’s look forward to it! Join us on February 24th at the NDHU Concert Hall, for a wonderful night of jazz. You are sincerely invited to be part of this unforgettable experience!
