2025年端午節連續假期垃圾清運時間調整，敬請留意。Adjustment of Garbage Collection Schedule during the 2025 Dragon Boat Festival Long Weekend

2025/05/20 08:14 ·
行政公告 ·
總務處環境保護組 info 
ㄧ、為配合端午節連續假期，本校垃圾清運時間將調整如下：
(一)114年5月30日(週五) 垃圾車將暫停清運服務一日。
(二)114年5月31日(週六) 垃圾清運時間調整如下：
1. 中午12:40至下午2:40：學生宿舍區。
2. 下午6:00至晚上7:20：教職員宿舍區。
3. 當日詳細班表請參閱附件。
二、敬請於垃圾停收期間勿將垃圾棄置於室外，造成您的不便，敬請見諒。
​
總務處環境保護組 敬啟
I. In observance of the Dragon Boat Festival long weekend, the university's garbage collection schedule will be adjusted as follows:
(I) Friday, May 30th, 2025: Garbage truck service will be suspended for one day.
(II) Saturday, May 31st, 2025: The garbage collection schedule will be adjusted as follows:
* 12:40 PM to 2:40 PM: Student Dormitory Area
* 6:00 PM to 7:20 PM: Faculty and Staff Dormitory Area
* Please refer to the attached document for the detailed schedule on this day.
II. We kindly request that you do not leave garbage outdoors during the service suspension period. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

Office of General Affairs Environmental Protection Division NDHU
