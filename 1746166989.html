國立東華大學公告

教務處公告--請大三同學於114/5/9-6/4在「學士班學程選課輔助及畢業初審系統」上傳課規年 Announcement of the Academic Affairs Office - Students who are Juniors please upload the course planning on the "Undergraduate Course Selection Assistance and Graduation Preliminary Examination System" before June 4th,2025.

2025/05/02 14:23 ·
行政公告 ·
教務處註冊組 info 
親愛同學大家好：
為協助同學順利畢業，請大三同學(累計六學期在學者)在下學期網路選課(初選)前，先於「學士班學程選課輔助及畢業初審系統」選定課規年及列印初審確認單送交學系。相關作業說明如下：
一、上傳課規年
(一)系統路徑：「學士班學程選課輔助及畢業初審系統」https://web.ndhu.edu.tw/aa/graduate/Login.aspx
(二)上傳對象：目前學士班大三在學生
(三)上傳期限：114/5/9(週五)上午8:00起至6/4(週三)中午12:30止(即網路初選截止)
(四)注意事項：
1、所選定上傳課規年包含主修、雙主修、輔系、副修及通識等，可分別採不同學年度；若上傳後發現不適用，請洽所屬學系申請取消鎖定課規，再重新上傳。
2、請於期限前於系統中印出「列印初審確認單(大三下用)」交至學系、學位學程助理彙辦。
3、倘學系有另行規劃上傳課規年度及印製初審確認單的時間，請學系應事先轉知所屬同學，並請同學依所屬學系公告之時間為準。
二、若對主修/雙主修/輔系/副修等學程的畢業學分有疑問者，請逕與相關學系、學位學程聯繫；
(一)校核心通識課程選課、抵免及程式設計能力：請洽通識中心(校內電話:03-8906602、6605、6607)
(二)英語選課及免修、英語能力檢定分級測驗：請洽語言中心(校內電話:03-8905492、5497)
(三)體育修課及體適能：請洽體育中心(校內電話:03-8906613)
(四)中文能力與涵養及華語課程：請洽華語文中心(校內電話:03-8905248)
(五)跨域自主學習：請洽生活輔導組(校內電話:03-8906227)
(六)行政單位及各系所電話：https://aa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/406-1004-210584,r1064.php

Dear junior students,
Please submit academic year, print the Initial Assessment by Graduate Admissions (Third-year Students) and send it to your department before Course Preliminary Selecting for Next Semester. 

The steps as follows: 
1. Submit academic year
(1) System:
Undergraduate Course Selection Assistance and Graduation Preliminary Examination System
https://web.ndhu.edu.tw/aa/graduate/Login.aspx 
(2) Effective period: From 8:00 a.m. on 114/5/9 (Friday) to 12:30 p.m. on 114/6/4 (Wednesday) (i.e., the deadline for Course Preliminary Selecting for Next Semester) 
(3) Precautions: 
i. The course planning includes majors, double majors, minors, minors and general course, etc., which can be selected for different academic years. If you want to change course planning after uploading, please contact your department to unlock and upload it again.
ii. Please print out the " Initial Assessment by Graduate Admissions (Third-year Students)" in the system before the deadline and submit it to the department. 
iii. If the department has different arrangements, the department should inform the students in advance, and the students should follow the announcement by the department.

2. If you have any questions about the graduation credits of majors/double majors/minors/programs, please contact the relevant departments. 
(1) General Education credits and Information Technology credits: please contact the General Education Center (Tel: 03-8906602 or 03-8906603、6605、6607).
(2) English course selection and exemption, English proficiency assessment and placement test: please contact the Language Center (Tel: 03-8905492、5497)
(3) Physical education course selection and physical fitness: please contact the Physical Education Center (Tel: 03-8906613).
(4) Chinese language proficiency and cultivation, as well as Mandarin courses: please contact the Chinese Language Center(Tel: 03-8905248).
(5) Interdisciplinary Autonomous Learning: please contact the Life Counseling Team (Tel: 03-8906227) 
(6) Administrative units and department contact numbers: https://aa.ndhu.edu.tw/p/406-1004-210584,r1064.php
